# Space scene assets

The Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and Neptune use meshes and imagery from NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD). Mars imagery is credited to NASA/JPL-Caltech. The Earth texture is the July 2004 Blue Marble Next Generation image by Reto Stöckli, NASA Earth Observatory.

[Mercury](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/mercury-3d-model/) · [Venus](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/venus-3d-model/) · [Earth](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography/) · [Mars](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/planet-mars-3d-model/) · [Jupiter](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/jupiter-3d-model/) · [Saturn](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/saturn-3d-model/) · [Neptune](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/neptune-3d-model/) · [Sun](https://science.nasa.gov/learn/heat/resource/sun-3d-model/)
